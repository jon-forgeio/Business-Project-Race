# Important
This is a household guide, not medical advice. For feeding, sleep, health or recovery questions, speak to your doctor, midwife or health visitor.

# Decide three rules before the baby arrives
Rules save decisions. Pick three and write them down:
- Visitors come for short visits, and they bring food or do one task.
- Each adult gets one protected sleep block per day, taking turns.
- The house does not need to be clean. It needs a clear path to the bed, the bathroom and the kitchen.

# A helpers list you can use today
Write down five people who offered help, and one job for each: a dinner, a load of laundry, a grocery run, pet care, or an older child's pickup. When someone asks "What can I do?", send them a line from the list instead of a vague answer.

# A visitor script
"Thank you for wanting to visit. We are keeping visits short for now. Would you be able to come on [day] between [time] and [time]? If you can bring [food or an errand], that would help. Please stay home if you feel unwell."

# A handoff note for whoever is on duty
- Last feed time and the next expected time
- Last diaper change
- Sleep notes from the last period
- Who to call with a question, and where the supplies are

# The 15-minute weekly reset
- Look at the week ahead: appointments, visitors, meals.
- Update the shopping list.
- Choose one thing to stop doing this week.
