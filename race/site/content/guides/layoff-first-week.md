# Day 1: stabilise (60 to 90 minutes)
- Write down your last day, severance terms and any benefits end dates.
- Save copies of reviews, project lists and contact details you are allowed to keep.
- List monthly bills and note which can pause or shrink.
- Tell three trusted people what happened in one sentence each.
# Day 2: inventory your wins
- List ten results from your last two roles. For each write: what you did, what changed, and a number (time saved, money, volume, speed).
- Pick the five you are proudest of. They become your resume bullets.
# A script for a former colleague
"Hi [Name], I hope you are well. My role at [Company] ended this month and I am looking for [target role]. Would you be open to a 15-minute call, or to pointing me at one person I should speak to? Happy to return the favour any time."
# Why this order
The goal of week one is not to apply everywhere. It is to stabilise, document what you did, and get your first conversations booked. Applications work better once the resume is rewritten and a few people know you are looking.
