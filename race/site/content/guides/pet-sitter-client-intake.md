# Why intake matters
Most problems with a pet visit are information problems: the wrong door code, an unmentioned allergy, a route the dog hates. A short intake prevents nearly all of them.
# The ten questions
- Pet name, species, breed, age and weight.
- Veterinarian name, clinic phone and address.
- What should I do in an emergency, and is there a spending limit you approve?
- Food: type, amount, times and where it is kept.
- Medications: name, dose, time and how your pet takes it.
- Walk routine: length, route, leash type, and any triggers such as other dogs, bikes or loud noises.
- Behaviours I should know: fear, chewing, escaping, guarding food or toys.
- Where are leashes, waste bags, towels and cleaning supplies?
- Door codes, alarm instructions and a neighbour contact.
- Dates and times you need care.
# Meet-and-greet checklist
- Arrive ten minutes early and let the pet approach you first.
- Walk the home with the owner: feeding spot, supplies, exits, hazards.
- Test the key or code before the owner leaves.
- Confirm the schedule, price and payment method in writing.
