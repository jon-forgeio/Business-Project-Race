# Why the last three days matter
Most moving stress comes from doing tasks in the wrong order: packing the kitchen before the first-night box exists, or booking utilities for the wrong date. A fixed sequence removes most of the guesswork.

# Three days out
- Confirm the mover's arrival window and parking access in writing.
- Put every box you will carry yourself in one spot: documents, medication, chargers, cash for tips.
- Empty the freezer into a cooler if the fridge will be unplugged.

# Two days out
- Pack the first-night box (see below) and label it on all sides.
- Take a dated photo or short video of each room, especially any existing damage.
- Give the new address to anyone delivering on moving day.

# The day before
- Strip beds you will sleep on, but leave the mattress covers on.
- Charge phones and keep chargers out of the boxes.
- Check the truck or mover's inventory list matches what you expect.

# First-night box (checklist)
- Toilet paper, soap, a towel, and a hand towel
- Phone chargers and one lamp or flashlight
- Bedding for one night and a change of clothes
- Basic tools: a screwdriver, a box cutter, and scissors
- Snacks and water
- Any medication and pet supplies

# A note on address changes
Change of address rules differ by country, state and region. Check your postal service's official site and your bank, insurer and employer directly. Do not rely on a checklist alone for legal or tax forms.
