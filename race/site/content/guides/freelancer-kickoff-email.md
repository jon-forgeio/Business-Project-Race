# Kickoff email
"Subject: Kickoff for [Project]. Thanks again for choosing to work together. Here is what happens next: 1) you send [items] by [date]; 2) I send the first draft on [date]; 3) you send feedback by [date]. I will update you every [day]. If anything changes on your side, please tell me early."
# Scope checklist
- Deliverables, quantities, formats and file types.
- Number of revision rounds, and how feedback is submitted.
- What is excluded. Write it down.
- Timeline: kickoff, first draft, feedback window, final.
- Payment schedule and what triggers each payment.
- Who owns the final files, and when ownership transfers.
# When the client asks for something new
"Thanks for the idea. That is outside the agreed scope. I can add it for [price] and it would move delivery to [date], or we can do it as a second phase after delivery. Which would you prefer?"
# When feedback is late
"I have not heard back on [item]. To keep the schedule I will wait until [date]. After that the delivery date moves by the same number of days."
