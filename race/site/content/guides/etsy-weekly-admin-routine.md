# Why a routine beats motivation
Digital shops drift. One week you refresh three listings, the next you do nothing and wonder why views are down. A short, boring routine keeps the shop moving.
# The weekly routine (30 minutes)
- Check messages, reviews, favourites and traffic.
- Reply to every review in one sentence.
- Note your top three listings by views and your bottom three by conversion.
- Pick ONE listing to improve this week: the first image, the title, or the tags. Only one.
- Log what you changed and the date so you can compare after two weeks.
# New-listing checklist
- Write the first 40 characters of the title to carry the main keyword: what it is and who it is for.
- Use all 13 tags: five exact phrases a buyer would type, four use-cases, two occasions, two formats.
- Description order: what you get, file formats and sizes, how to download, what is not included, usage terms.
- Upload 10 images: cover, what is included, mockup in use, format list, preview, FAQ, bundle cross-sell, instructions, closing image.
- After publishing, open the listing as a buyer and download the file.
# Common mistakes
- Changing five things at once, so you cannot tell what worked.
- Cover images with more than five words of text.
- Delivery files that were never opened on a phone.
