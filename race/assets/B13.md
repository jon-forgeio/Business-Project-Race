# Important
This guide covers household organisation only. It is not medical advice. For any question about your baby's feeding, sleep, health, or your own recovery, ask your doctor, midwife or health visitor.
# The idea
In the first month, the household runs on small, repeatable routines and on help from others. Decide a few simple rules now so you do not have to decide every day.
# Week 1: Keep it simple
- Daily: eat something, drink water, rest when the baby rests.
- One task per day: laundry, dishes, or tidy a room, not all at once.
- Visitors: only short visits, and only if they bring food or help.
- Stock up: ready meals, snacks, toilet paper, laundry detergent.
# Week 2: Add a rhythm
- Pick two moments a day for a short tidy (morning and evening).
- Plan three simple dinners for the week and write them on a list.
- Ask one person to do a weekly grocery run.
# Week 3: Share the load
- Make a shared list of tasks and who does what.
- Plan one outing of any kind, even a short walk, if you feel up to it.
- Plan one longer sleep block for each adult, taking turns.
# Week 4: Look ahead
- Review what is working and what is not.
- Decide how to handle returning to work, childcare or help at home.
- Book a date with each other, even a short one at home.
# Helpers list
- Write down five people who offered help and what each one could do: meals, laundry, errands, pet care, older children.
- When someone asks "What can I do?", send them a task from this list.
# Visitor script (copy and paste)
"Thank you so much for wanting to visit. We would love to see you, and we are keeping visits short for now. Would you be able to come on [day] between [time] and [time]? If you can bring [food/an errand], that would help a lot. Please stay home if you feel unwell."
# Meal-train message
"We are so grateful for the support. If you would like to help, here is a list of dates for dinners: [dates]. We eat [foods], and avoid [allergies]. Please leave meals on the porch in a disposable container. No need to stay to chat."
# Handoff note for a partner or caregiver
- Last feed time and next expected time.
- Last diaper change.
- Sleep notes.
- What the baby likes right now.
- Who to call if you have a question.
- Where the supplies are.
# Weekly reset (15 minutes)
- Look at the week ahead: appointments, visitors, meals.
- Update the shopping list.
- Choose one thing you will let go of this week.
