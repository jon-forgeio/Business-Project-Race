# How to use this
Count back from your move date. If you have less than eight weeks, compress the early weeks and do the same things in less time.
# Week 8
- Set a budget and decide whether you will hire movers or do it yourself.
- Begin sorting by room: keep, sell, donate, recycle.
- Collect quotes from at least two moving companies if hiring.
# Week 7
- Book movers or reserve a truck. Confirm dates in writing.
- Order boxes, tape, markers and packing paper, or ask friends and local shops for boxes.
- Start a moving folder for receipts and documents.
# Week 6
- Notify your landlord or begin the sale paperwork as needed.
- Make a list of everyone who needs your new address.
- Start packing items you use rarely: books, decor, off-season clothes.
# Week 5
- Request time off work for moving day.
- Schedule disconnect and connect dates for utilities and internet.
- Arrange childcare or pet care for moving day.
# Week 4
- Submit an official address change with the postal service.
- Update address for bank, cards, insurance, subscriptions and employer.
- Pack a second round of rooms: guest room, garage, storage.
# Week 3
- Confirm utilities start dates at the new home.
- Photograph the condition of your current home.
- Use up the food in the freezer and pantry.
# Week 2
- Pack most remaining rooms. Label every box with room and contents.
- Confirm the mover's arrival time and parking access.
- Pack valuables and documents to carry yourself.
# Week 1
- Pack a "first-night" box (see below).
- Defrost the freezer and clean the fridge.
- Do a final cleaning plan and gather keys and codes.
# Moving day
- Walk through every room and closet before leaving.
- Take final meter readings or photos.
- Keep your valuables and the first-night box with you.
- Check the truck before the movers leave.
# Packing order
- First: books, decor, spare linens.
- Then: kitchen items you do not need daily.
- Last: bathroom, bedding, daily dishes.
# Address-change checklist
- Postal service, bank and credit cards, insurance, employer, doctor and dentist, subscriptions, school, friends and family, voter registration, vehicle registration and licence (rules vary by location, so check local requirements).
# First-night kit
- Toilet paper, soap, towels, phone chargers, bedding, a change of clothes, basic tools, snacks, water, pet supplies, medications, a lamp or flashlight.
