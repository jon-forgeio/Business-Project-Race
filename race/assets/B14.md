# Note
Check local rules before any league involves money or prizes. Keep it friendly and fair.
# League constitution (copy and edit)
- League name, sport, platform, season dates.
- Number of teams, roster size, starting lineup, scoring settings.
- Draft type and date, draft order method.
- Waiver rules: day of week, priority, free-agent rules.
- Trade rules: deadline, review process, veto rule.
- Playoffs: how many teams, weeks, tiebreakers.
- Dues: amount, due date, who holds them, what happens if unpaid.
- Prize structure, if any.
- Conduct: respect, no collusion, no abandoning a team.
- Commissioner role: how rule changes are decided (for example majority vote before the draft).
# Draft-night plan
- One week before: confirm date, send a reminder with the link and draft order.
- Day before: remind everyone to review rules and set rankings.
- Draft night: start time, pick clock length, what happens if someone misses a pick (auto-draft).
- After the draft: post the rosters, the waiver schedule and the first-week reminder.
# Trade and waiver policy
- Trades must be posted in the league chat.
- Trades are reviewed for 24 hours. A trade is blocked only with a majority of other managers voting no for a clear fairness reason.
- No trading draft picks for money outside the league rules.
- Waiver order resets weekly or by priority. Write down which one you use.
# Tiebreakers
- Regular season: head-to-head record, then total points for.
- Playoffs: the higher seed, then total points for.
- Bracket ties: the team with more bench points wins, then a coin flip with a witness.
# Weekly recap template
- Headline of the week.
- Game of the week.
- Best pickup, worst decision, power rankings (top 3).
- Next week's must-watch matchup.

# Ten dispute rules
- Raise issues in writing within 48 hours.
- The commissioner decides based on the written rules.
- If the commissioner is involved, another manager decides.
- Rule changes mid-season require a majority vote.
- No retroactive changes unless there was a clear error.
- Keep all trade and waiver messages in the league chat.
- A manager who leaves must give notice and help find a replacement.
- Repeated inactivity may lead to removal.
- Disagreements about scoring corrections follow the platform's official stats.
- Be polite. It is a game.
