# How to use this pack
Copy each section into your notes app or email and edit the brackets. Keep a saved copy of every client's completed intake so you can reuse it.
# 1. Client intake questionnaire (16 questions)
- Owner name, phone, best way to reach you, and a backup contact.
- Home address and where to park.
- Pet name, species, breed, age, and weight.
- Is your pet spayed or neutered, and up to date on vaccines?
- Veterinarian name, clinic phone, and address.
- What should I do in an emergency, and is there a spending limit you approve?
- Food: type, amount, times, and where it is kept.
- Treats allowed, and any foods to avoid.
- Medications: name, dose, time, how your pet takes it.
- Walk routine: length, route, leash type, and any triggers (other dogs, bikes, noises).
- Behaviours I should know: fear, chewing, escaping, resource guarding.
- Where are leashes, waste bags, towels, and cleaning supplies?
- Where does your pet sleep and how do you settle them?
- Alarm system, door codes, and neighbour contact.
- Dates and times you need care.
- Anything else that would help me care for your pet the way you do.
# 2. Meet-and-greet checklist
- Arrive 10 minutes early. Let the pet approach you first.
- Walk the home with the owner: feeding spot, supplies, exits, hazards.
- Test the key or code before the owner leaves.
- Confirm the schedule, price and payment method in writing.
- Take notes on anything fragile or off-limits.
# 3. Pet care sheet (to leave in the home)
- Pet name, vet and emergency number, owner contact, sitter contact.
- Feeding schedule and amounts, medication schedule.
- Walk times and route, and any special commands.
- Where the supplies are and where to put waste.
# 4. Five daily update messages
- Arrival: "Hi [Name], I am here with [Pet]. Starting with [food/walk]."
- Walk update: "Just finished a [length] walk. [Pet] had a great time and [one detail]."
- Meal update: "[Pet] ate [all/most] of the [meal]. Water refreshed."
- Medication update: "Gave [medication] at [time]. No problem."
- Departure: "All done for today. [Pet] is settled with [toy/bed]. See you [next visit time]."
# 5. Policies page
- Booking: how far ahead, and your deposit if any.
- Cancellation: how much notice, and what you charge for late cancellations.
- Emergencies: you will contact the owner first, then the vet, and you will follow the approved spending limit.
- Keys and codes: how they are stored and returned.
- Photos: you may share photos only with the owner unless they allow otherwise.
- Aggression or safety: you can end a visit if you feel unsafe.
# 6. Post-visit thank-you
"Thank you for trusting me with [Pet]. They were a joy. If you are happy with the visits, a short review or a recommendation to a friend would mean a lot."
