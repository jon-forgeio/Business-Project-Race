# What this is
A set of copy-and-paste templates for the first week of a client project and the last day. It is not a contract. Have your own agreement reviewed by a qualified professional where needed.
# 1. Intake questionnaire (20 questions)
- What problem are we solving, in one sentence?
- Who is it for?
- What happens if nothing changes in 3 months?
- What does a great result look like and how will you measure it?
- Is there a deadline and what is driving it?
- What have you tried before?
- Who makes the final decision and who else needs to review?
- What are your must-haves and nice-to-haves?
- Which examples do you like and why?
- Which examples do you dislike and why?
- What assets exist (logos, files, accounts, copy)?
- Who gives me access to those?
- What is the budget range?
- How do you prefer to communicate (email, calls, chat) and how often?
- What are your working hours and time zone?
- What topics are off-limits?
- Is there anyone else I should speak to?
- What would make you unhappy by the end of the project?
- How did you hear about me?
- Anything else I should know?
# 2. Scope checklist
- Deliverables, quantity, format and file types.
- Number of revisions included and how they are submitted.
- What is excluded (write it down).
- Timeline: kickoff, first draft, feedback window, final.
- Payment schedule and what triggers each payment.
- Who owns the final files and when ownership transfers.
# 3. Kickoff email
Subject: Kickoff for [Project]. Hi [Name], thanks again for choosing to work together. Here is what happens next: 1) you send [items] by [date]; 2) I send [first draft] on [date]; 3) you share feedback by [date]. I will update you every [day] by [time]. If anything changes on your side, please tell me early.
# 4. Weekly status template
- Done this week: [list]. In progress: [list]. Needed from you: [list with dates]. Risks: [list]. Next week: [list].
# 5. Change-request reply
"Thanks for the idea. This is outside the agreed scope. I can add it for [price] and it would move the delivery to [date]. If you prefer, we can do it as a second phase after delivery. Which would you like?"
# 6. Late feedback reply
"I have not heard back on [item]. To keep the schedule I will wait until [date]. After that the delivery date moves by the same number of days."
# 7. Offboarding and handoff checklist
- Deliver final files with a summary of what is included.
- Share login details through a secure method, never plain email.
- Provide a one-page how-to or notes.
- Confirm final payment, invoice and receipt.
- Archive project files and set a date to delete client data.
# 8. Testimonial request
"Thanks for working together. If you were happy, would you write two or three sentences about the result and what it was like to work with me? Here are three prompts: what problem did I solve, what surprised you, who would you recommend me to?"
# 9. Follow-up (30 days later)
"Quick check: how is [deliverable] working? Anything I can tune? If you plan a next project I would be glad to talk."
