# How to use this pack
Copy a message, replace the [brackets], and send. Keep a note app open with your property details (address, access method, wifi, parking, quiet hours) so replies take seconds. Messages are short on purpose: guests skim.
# Stage 1: Inquiry
- Quick reply: "Hi [Name], thanks for asking about [dates]. They are available. The home sleeps [N] and is [short selling point]. Let me know if I can answer anything before you book."
- Dates not available: "Thanks for reaching out. Those dates are taken, but [alternate dates] are open. Happy to hold them for 24 hours."
- Pet or party question: "We [do/do not] allow pets. Parties and events are not permitted, but a quiet dinner for guests registered on the booking is fine."
- Discount request: "Thanks for asking. Our rate reflects [reason]. For stays of [N]+ nights I can offer [discount]."
# Stage 2: Booking confirmed
- "Welcome, [Name]! Your stay is confirmed for [dates]. I will send arrival details [N] days before. In the meantime, here is what makes the area special: [two tips]."
- Ask for ETA: "To help me plan, what time do you expect to arrive?"
# Stage 3: Pre-arrival (2 to 3 days before)
- Arrival details: address, parking, access method, wifi network and password, quiet hours.
- Local tips: one coffee shop, one dinner place, one grocery, one family activity.
- Weather and packing note.
# Stage 4: Check-in day
- "Hi [Name], the home is ready. Door code is [code]. Check-in is from [time]. If anything is unclear, message me here."
- Late arrival: "No problem. The code works any time. I will leave the lights on."
# Stage 5: Mid-stay
- Day 1 check: "Hope you settled in well. Anything missing or unclear?"
- Longer stay check: "Checking in. Do you need extra towels or supplies?"
# Stage 6: Problems
- Something broke: "I am sorry about that. I will [fix/replace] by [time]. If it is urgent, call [number]."
- Noise complaint from neighbours: "Neighbours have mentioned some noise. Could you keep it down after [time]? Thank you for helping us stay good neighbours."
- Cleanliness complaint: "I am sorry. Could you send a photo? I will arrange a fix today and can offer [partial refund/credit] if appropriate."
- Lockout: "Try code [backup]. If that fails call [number]. I can send a locksmith if needed."
# Stage 7: Checkout
- "Checkout is at [time]. Please [stripped list: start dishwasher, take out trash, lock door]. Thank you for being a great guest."
- Late checkout request: "I can offer until [time] for [fee], or you are welcome to leave bags in the hall."
# Stage 8: Review request
- "Thanks for staying, [Name]! If you enjoyed the home, a short review would mean a lot. I will review you too."
# Stage 9: Repeat guest
- "Thanks again for staying with us. If you are planning another trip, I can offer [direct-rebook perk]."
# House manual skeleton
- Wifi, TV, thermostat, parking, trash day, quiet hours, emergency contacts, local rules, checkout steps, where supplies are, appliance notes.
# Issue and cancellation guide
- Decide in advance: partial refund thresholds, maintenance response times, cancellation rules, deposit rules. Write them once and paste when needed.
