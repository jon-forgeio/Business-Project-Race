# The rule before you pitch
Listen to at least two episodes first. Pitch only shows where your topic helps their audience. One personalised pitch is worth more than twenty copied ones.
# Subject lines (12)
- Guest idea for [Show]: [topic] in [number] minutes
- A fresh angle on [topic] for your listeners
- Pitch: what [audience] gets wrong about [topic]
- Listener question your guests have not answered: [question]
- [Your name] on [topic] (and a story about [detail])
- Idea for an episode about [topic]
- Loved your episode on [episode topic]; a related idea
- Guest suggestion: [topic]
- For [host name]: three talking points on [topic]
- Short pitch: [topic] and [benefit]
- Would [topic] fit your show?
- [Mutual connection] suggested I reach out
# Pitch 1: The topic-first pitch
Hi [Host], I enjoyed your episode on [episode], especially [one specific detail]. I work on [your area] and could speak about [topic]: 1) [talking point], 2) [talking point], 3) [talking point]. Here is a one-line bio: [bio]. If it fits your show, I would be glad to share more. Thanks for the great work.
# Pitch 2: The listener-problem pitch
Hi [Host], your listeners seem to care about [problem]. I have helped [type of person] with it by [approach]. A possible episode: "[title]". I can bring a short, practical checklist for your audience. Happy to send links to my past interviews.
# Pitch 3: The story pitch
Hi [Host], a quick story: [two-sentence story]. It taught me [lesson], which I think would resonate with your audience. I would love to share it on your show.
# Pitch 4: The mutual connection pitch
Hi [Host], [Name] suggested I reach out. We were talking about [topic], and they thought your listeners would like it. If you are booking guests, here are three talking points: [bullets].
# Pitch 5: The book or launch pitch
Hi [Host], I have a [book/launch] coming on [date] about [topic]. I do not want to just promote it. I would rather give your listeners a useful framework from it: [one-sentence framework]. Would that suit an upcoming episode?
# Pitch 6: The short pitch (under 70 words)
Hi [Host], I am [name], [one-line credential]. I would love to talk about [topic] with your listeners. Three ideas: [a], [b], [c]. Can I send details?
# Bio formula (one paragraph)
[Name] helps [audience] [result] through [method]. Previously [credential]. Find [him/her/them] at [link].
# Follow-up sequence
- Day 7: "Just checking in on my note about [topic]. Happy to send a short outline."
- Day 14: "One more nudge. If it is not right for the show, no problem. Thank you for your time."
- Day 30: send a new angle or a useful resource, no ask.
# After recording
- Send a thank-you within 24 hours with two or three shareable quotes and the link you want them to use.
