# Channel plans per business (2026-11-26)

Active channels: Netlify landing site per business, Gumroad store (linked accounts, see FIVE_BUSINESSES_PLAN.md), DEV, Mastodon, SEO. Facebook and Reddit are parked. Hashnode is out (paid plan needed).

Rules that apply to every business: disclose that a business is run with AI assistance where a platform asks, use no fake reviews or testimonials, use no invented people, and link each post to the business's free guide first.

## Second Draft Careers (B03, B05, B10, B15)
- Site: a clean, editorial look (serif headings, warm neutral palette). Home shows the four products and two free guides.
- DEV: one article per week on resume bullets or the first week after a layoff, linking to the matching product.
- Mastodon: up to 2 posts a day, each a concrete tip with one link to a free guide.
- SEO: target job-search and resume-bullet terms. Titles and meta descriptions per product page.

## Hearth & Host (B01, B04, B09, B12, B13)
- Site: a warm, approachable look (rounded cards, soft colors). Home groups products by hosting, pets, moving and newborns.
- DEV: one article a week on hosting checklists or moving timelines.
- Mastodon: up to 2 posts a day with a usable checklist or message template.
- SEO: terms for wedding toasts, guest messages, pet sitting forms and moving checklists.

## Solo Ops Studio (B02, B06, B08)
- Site: a crisp, technical look (grid layout, monospace accents). Home shows SOP packs, listing rewrites and onboarding packs.
- DEV: one article a week on Etsy SOPs or client onboarding steps.
- Mastodon: up to 2 posts a day with a checklist item or listing example.
- SEO: Etsy listing and freelancer onboarding terms.

## Classroom & Family Desk (B07, B11)
- Site: a friendly, classroom-style look (bright accents, simple layout). Home shows teacher prompts and the scholarship outliner.
- DEV: one article a week on parent emails or scholarship essay structure.
- Mastodon: up to 2 posts a day with a prompt or outline example.
- SEO: teacher-communication and scholarship essay terms.

## Game Night Co (B14)
- Site: a playful look (bold colors, game-board motifs). One product, so the site is a single page plus a free guide.
- DEV: one article on commissioner rules or draft-night setup, if the product earns it.
- Mastodon: up to 1 post a day.
- SEO: fantasy league commissioner terms. Consider merging into Hearth & Host if traffic stays at zero.
