1.
Commissioner tip: put a "Changed this season" box at the top of your league rules. Three lines, max. Returning managers skim, and this is the part they need.

2.
One rule that ends a lot of trade fights before they start:

"A trade is reversed only for clear collusion, and the reason is posted in the league chat."

Bad judgment is part of the game. Collusion is not.

Five more rules like this in a free guide: https://sprintshelf.netlify.app/guides/league-commissioner-rules/

3.
Draft night, one week out:
[ ] Date and time confirmed by everyone
[ ] Pick timer written down
[ ] Draft order posted
[ ] Absentee rule posted
[ ] Roster settings checked

Roll the dice early, not at pick one.

#FantasyDraft #FantasyFootball

4.
"Week 10" is not a trade deadline.
"Wednesday of week 10, 11:59 p.m. Eastern" is.

Day, time, time zone. Every time.

#FantasyCommissioner

5.
When a dispute lands in the group chat, don't rule in the first hour. Reply with:

"Got it, I'll look into it. Details in a separate thread. Decision by tomorrow evening."

Acknowledge, lower the audience, set a deadline.

#FantasyCommissioner #FantasyFootball

6.
Post every ruling in three parts:

Decision: the trade stands.
Rule: 3.3, reversals only for collusion.
Reason: no evidence of a side deal.

No sarcasm, no commentary. Just the board state.

#FantasyCommissioner

7.
Tiebreaker order worth writing down before week one:
1. Total points for
2. Head-to-head record
3. Points against
4. Random draw

Numbered tiebreakers are much harder to argue with than "we'll figure it out."

#FantasyFootball #FantasyCommissioner

8.
Every rulebook needs a fallback line:

"Not covered here? The platform default applies. No default? The commissioner decides and writes a rule for next season."

One sentence, many arguments avoided.

9.
Stat corrections: pick one and post it.

A) Corrections always apply until the platform finalizes the week.
B) Matchups lock when the next week's first game starts.

Either works. Silence does not.

#FantasyFootball #FantasyCommissioner

10.
Name a backup commissioner before draft night. If your connection drops, someone needs to be able to pause the clock. Tell them before the draft, not during it.

#FantasyDraft #FantasyCommissioner

11.
Ask every manager for a thumbs-up that they read page one of the rules. Thirty seconds for them. A much calmer season for you.

#FantasyCommissioner #FantasyFootball

12.
Trade-back rule to stop "rent a player for a week" moves:

"The same two players can't be traded back between the same two teams for 4 weeks."

#FantasyFootball #FantasyCommissioner

13.
Commissioners: if a dispute involves your own team, hand it off. Name a co-commissioner in the rules before the season. Ruling on your own case, even correctly, will look wrong to someone.

16.
Scoring explainer trick: translate settings into sentences.

Not "REC: 0.5."
Instead: "Each catch is worth half a point, on top of yards and touchdowns."

Say it out loud. If it sounds clear, it is clear.

#FantasyFootball

17.
Draft-night undo rule:

"One undo per manager per draft, only before the next pick is made."

Fat-finger insurance, with a limit.

1. Any rule that felt unclear?
2. Anything to change next season?
3. Reminder: trade deadline is [date].

Collect notes now. Change rules next year, not mid-season.
