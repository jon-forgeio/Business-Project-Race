## 1
Moving soon? Start one note called "Move" with three headings: To do, To buy, To tell. Every time something pops into your head, it goes under one of them. Future you will be grateful.

## 2
Open-first box for moving day (travels with you, not on the truck):
- chargers
- toilet paper and soap
- a towel
- tomorrow's clothes
- kettle, mugs, snacks
- scissors and a screwdriver
- bedding for night one

## 4
Label moving boxes with the room AND a short list of contents.
"Kitchen" is a guess.
"Kitchen: baking tins, cookbooks, measuring jugs" is an answer.
Number them too, so you notice if one goes missing.

#Moving #Organizing

## 5
Short-term rental hosts: one message worth saving.
"Hi [Name], the home is ready. Door code is [code]. Check-in is from [time]. If anything is unclear, message me here."
Send arrival details two to three days ahead, then this on the day.

#AirbnbHost #Hosting

## 6
First-morning message for guests:
"Good morning, [Name]. I hope you settled in well. Is there anything missing or anything I can help with?"
It catches the small stuff (a missing towel, a confusing shower) before it becomes a disappointing stay.

#Hosting #ShortTermRental

## 8
When something breaks during a guest's stay, a calm reply has three parts:
1. Acknowledge it and say thanks for telling you.
2. Say what you will do and by when.
3. Give a number for anything urgent.
That is it. Quick beats perfect.

#Hosting #ShortTermRental

## 9
Weekend guests coming? Guest room check, the day before:
- fresh sheets and a spare blanket
- towels left on the bed
- a few free hangers
- a working bedside lamp
- a charger near a free socket
- a glass for water

#Hosting #Checklist

## 10
The kindest sentence you can say to house guests: "Please help yourself to tea, coffee and snacks."
Guests often feel awkward asking. Say it once on arrival and the whole weekend gets easier.

#Hosting

## 11
Leaving a pet with a sitter? Put the contacts at the top of the care sheet, not the bottom:
- your number
- a nearby backup contact
- your vet
- the nearest emergency vet
And decide in advance what the sitter should do in an emergency.

#Pets #PetSitting

## 13
A simple daily update template for pet sitters:
"Visit done at [time]. [Pet] ate [all/some] of breakfast, had a [length] walk, and is now [napping/playing]. Anything to flag: [none/note]."
Owners get peace of mind. You get a routine.

#PetSitting #Pets

## 14
Meet-and-greet tip for pet sitters: test the key or door code while the owner is still there. A code that "should work" is not the same as one that does.

## 15
Wedding toast rule of thumb: one story, not five. Two to three minutes, about 300 to 400 words read aloud. Pick the memory that makes you smile while you write it, and cut the rest.

#Wedding #WeddingToast

## 16
Stuck on the first line of a wedding toast? Try:
"For those who do not know me, I am the person who knows too much and has promised to say too little."
Then say your name and how you know the couple, in one sentence.

#WeddingToast #PublicSpeaking

## 18
A visitor message for new parents, ready to send:
"We are so glad you want to meet the baby. We are taking the first couple of weeks quietly and will let you know when we are ready for visitors. Thank you for understanding."

#NewParents #Parenting

## 19
A weekly 15-minute reset for the newborn weeks:
1. Clear one surface.
2. Check the fridge and write a short list.
3. Restock nappies, wipes and basics.
4. Look at next week's appointments and visitors.
Not a tidy house. Just fewer surprises.
