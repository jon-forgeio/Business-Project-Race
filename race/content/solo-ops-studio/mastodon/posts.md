# Solo Ops Studio: Mastodon posts

1. Shop SOP, step 1 of any packing table: print today's orders sorted by ship-by date, oldest first. Then pick, check the variant against the slip, and only then reach for a box. Order of operations beats speed. #SmallBusiness #Etsy

2. Listing title order that keeps it readable:
1) what it is
2) key attribute (material, size, format)
3) who it is for or the occasion
Brand name last, only if there is room.
Free checklist: https://jon-forgeio.github.io/Business-Project-Race/solo-ops-studio/ #Etsy #EtsySeller

3. Weekly admin rule: change ONE thing on ONE listing. First image, title, tags, or the first two lines of the description. Not all four. Otherwise you cannot tell what made the difference. #Etsy #SmallBusiness

4. Freelancer kickoff checklist item most worth protecting:
[ ] Final decision-maker named
Feedback from someone who was never in the loop is how projects stall. #Freelance #Freelancing

5. Listing example.
Before: "The Wanderer | Handmade Card Sleeve"
After: "Leather Card Holder, Slim Minimalist Wallet for 4 Cards, Hand Stitched Gift for Him"
Same item. The second one says what it is. #Etsy #EtsySeller

6. Delivery file QA for digital shops:
[ ] Open every file on a phone
[ ] Open it on a laptop
[ ] Check fonts, margins, page count
[ ] Test the zip after zipping
Ten minutes per new listing. #Etsy #DigitalProducts

7. Scope checklist line people skip: "What is excluded." Write it down. Read it aloud at kickoff. It is much easier to agree before work starts than to argue about it later.
Free kickoff email + scope checklist: https://jon-forgeio.github.io/Business-Project-Race/solo-ops-studio/ #Freelance

8. 13 Etsy tags, one split that avoids repeats:
5 product phrases
3 material or style
3 buyer or occasion
2 use or format
Each tag a phrase a buyer would type, each one different. #Etsy #EtsySeller

9. Pre-batch packing check, before order one:
[ ] Spare label roll within reach
[ ] Tape gun full
[ ] 5+ of each box size
[ ] Inserts printed
[ ] Scale reads zero
Most packing delays are a missing supply halfway through. #SmallBusiness

10. Late feedback reply, copy and edit:
"I have not heard back on [item]. To keep the schedule I will wait until [date]. After that the delivery date moves by the same number of days."
Agree the rule at kickoff, then this email is just a reminder. #Freelance #Freelancing

11. A 30-minute weekly routine for a digital-download shop: messages, reviews, three best and three weakest listings, one improvement, one log line. Written up here, free: https://jon-forgeio.github.io/Business-Project-Race/solo-ops-studio/ #Etsy #SmallBusiness

12. Listing description, six blocks in this order:
1 Opening two lines
2 What you get
3 Specs
4 How it works
5 Not included
6 Good to know
Buyers scan for answers. Put them where they look. #Etsy #Ecommerce

13. Onboarding packet folder, duplicated per client:
00-start-here
01-intake
02-scope
03-how-we-work
04-access-and-assets
05-schedule
06-invoices
Numbered so the order never changes. #Freelance #Productivity

14. Change log for your shop, four columns: date, listing, what changed, check again on. Set the check date two weeks out. Without the date you will not remember what you touched. #Etsy #SmallBusiness

15. Kickoff, after the call:
[ ] Recap email within 24h: goal, scope, people, dates, next steps
It turns a conversation into something both sides can point at. #Freelance #ProjectManagement

17. Listing description check: the first two lines should answer what it is, size or quantity, and why someone would choose it. If your title says "fits 4 cards", line one should say it too. #Etsy #Ecommerce

[ ] Never take logins in plain email
[ ] Note how access is removed at the end
